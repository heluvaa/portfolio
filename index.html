01About Me

I'm a faceless builder. No personal brand of my face. The brand is shipped things. I design, build, deploy and maintain web apps, Telegram bots, and digital products end to end, solo.

How I work

  • Ship, then harden: every project gets deployed, then audited and fixed.
  • Verified over claimed: links on this page were HTTP-audited, numbers come from public APIs.
  • Small, fast, maintainable: static-first stacks, minimal infrastructure, full ownership.

Collaboration

  • Async-first, Telegram-native. Brief in, working product out.
  • Scope is written down before the first commit; progress is visible as URLs, not status reports.
  • Post-handover: I keep what I ship running, or document it so you can.
